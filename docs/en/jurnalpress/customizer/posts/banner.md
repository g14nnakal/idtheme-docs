# Banner After Content (Module) – JurnalPress Theme

The **Banner** section in the **Single Posts** panel displays banners (ads, images, or custom HTML) after article content. This section does not configure banners directly; it acts as a helper linking to the main banner settings.

## Lokasi Pengaturan
```
Appearance → Customize → Single Posts → After Content Element → Banner
```

## General Description

Banner After Content is typically used to:

- Display ads after the article is read
- Insert promotional banners
- Add specific HTML elements (CTAs, ad scripts, etc.)

This section displays a **notification** that directs users to the relevant banner settings page.

## Opsi yang Tersedia

### Notification Banner After Content

This is an informational notification, not an input field.

**Fungsi utama:**

- Inform users that banners are configured in another panel
- Provide a quick link to the corresponding banner settings

**Detail Teknis:**

- **Type**: Notification / Content
- **Tab**: General
- **Target Link**: No page reload (noreload)

**Pesan Notifikasi:**

> Note: To add banner after content element, please go to Customizer → Banner → After Content Element Banner. Insert your image or html in there.

**Tautan Aksi:**

- *Please go to banner settings*
- Points to: **Banner → After Content Element Banner**

## Alur Penggunaan

1. Open the **Customizer**
2. Choose **Single Posts**
3. Open **After Content Element**
4. Go to the **Banner** section
5. Click **Please go to banner settings**
6. Add an image or HTML banner in the Banner After Content Element settings

## Catatan Penting

- This section **does not store banner content**
- All banner settings are managed in the **Banner** main panel
- This section acts only as a helper linking to the main banner settings

## Kapan Perlu Menggunakan Section Ini?

Use Banner After Content if:

- You want to display ads after the article
- You need an additional CTA at the end of content
- You aim to optimize monetization or internal promotions
