# Share & Google News (Single Post) – JurnalPress Theme

The **Share & Google News** section configures social share buttons and adds a custom Google News URL on **Single Post** pages.  
These settings let you choose share platforms, display positions, and visual styles for share icons.

## Lokasi Pengaturan
```
Customizer → Single Posts → Share & Google News
```

## Tabs

This section has two main tabs:

- **General** → Functional and position settings for share buttons
- **Style** → Visual appearance settings for share icons

## General Settings

### 1. Share Button

Choose and configure the social platforms to display.

**Key features:**
- **Drag & drop** to reorder buttons
- **(+)** button to add share platforms
- **Facebook cannot be removed**
- Each platform has a **Hide on Mobile** option

**Available platforms:**
- Facebook
- Twitter
- Telegram
- Pinterest
- LinkedIn
- LINE
- WhatsApp
- Email

**Per‑platform option:**
- **Hide on Mobile**  
  Hide the share button on mobile devices.

### 2. Google News URL

Add your Google News publisher URL.

**Field:**
- **Google News Url**

**Example:**
- `https://news.google.com/publisher/xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx`

This URL is typically used for integration or as a Google News publisher marker.

### 3. Before Content & After Title (Post)

Enable share buttons **below the post title and before the content**.

- Default: **Enabled**
- Good for increasing share button visibility early in the reading flow

### 4. After Content (Post)

Display share buttons **after the article content**.

- Default: **Enabled**
- Commonly used as a call‑to‑action after readers finish the article

## Style Settings

### 1. Color Type

Choose the color source for share icons.

**Choices:**
- **Default**  
  Use the default color of each social platform
- **Custom**  
  Use user‑defined manual colors

### 2. Color

(Enabled only if **Color Type = Custom**)

Manual color settings for share icons:

- **Icon** → Warna ikon
- **Background** → Warna latar ikon

### 3. Style

Choose the visual style for share icons.

**Style choices:**
- **Background** → Icons with colored background
- **No Background** → Icons without background
- **Border** → Icons with border

### 4. Border Radius

Adjust the corner radius of share icons.

- Supports per‑side control (top, right, bottom, left)
- Larger values produce more rounded icons

### 5. Padding

Adjust spacing between the icon and its background or border.

- Useful to increase the clickable area
- Helps achieve proportional appearance

### 6. Icon Size

Set the share icon size.

- Minimum: **10px**
- Maksimum: **40px**

### 7. Gap

Set spacing between share icons.

- Minimum: **5px**
- Maksimum: **30px**
- Berguna untuk tampilan desktop maupun mobile

## Catatan Tambahan

- All changes can be previewed in **real time** in the Customizer
- Position combinations (before & after content) can be used together
- **Default Color** is recommended for consistency with social platform branding

## Ringkasan

The **Share & Google News** section provides full control over:
- Platform share sosial media
- Posisi tombol share pada single post
- Tampilan visual ikon share
- Integrasi URL Google News

These settings are designed to improve **engagement**, **sharing**, and **content distribution** for your articles. By optimizing share buttons and Google News, you can increase visibility and user interaction with your content.
