# How to Purchase a Theme

This guide explains step‑by‑step how to buy a theme from Kentooz / Idtheme.

## 1. Choose a Theme

1. Open the product page of the theme you’re interested in (e.g., Muvipro, Majalahpro, etc.).
2. In the pricing table, click **Buy Theme**.
3. You will be redirected to the purchase form on the Kentooz website.

The buy button may look different for each product, but it is generally located in the pricing/package section. Example:

![Example Buy Theme Button](/keanggotaan/beli-theme/tombol-beli-theme.jpg)

## 2. Fill Out the Purchase Form / Create an Account

On the purchase page:

- If you don’t have an account:
  - Fill in your details in the registration form (name, email, etc.).
  - Create a password for your Kentooz account.
- If you already have an account:
  - Click **Login** first, then continue selecting products.

You can:
- Switch the language to Indonesian (if available).
- Log in/sign up using Facebook.
- Choose the product/theme you want to buy (it may be a list, popup, or radio buttons depending on the layout).
- Enter a **coupon code** if you have one.
- Check the Anti‑SPAM option if requested.

After all data is correct, click **Next** to proceed to payment.

## 3. Choose a Payment Method

Typically, several payment methods are available:

- **PayPal**
  - After clicking Next, you will be redirected to PayPal.
  - Log in to your PayPal account and complete the payment as instructed.
  - If payment succeeds, your account is usually activated automatically and you can download the theme from the member area:  
    https://member.kentooz.com/member
  - If the account is not activated automatically, contact support for verification.

- **Offline Payment (Bank Transfer)**
  - You will be redirected to the offline payment instructions page.
  - You will also receive a **Pending Invoice** email from Kentooz (check the SPAM folder if it’s not in the inbox).

## 4. Pay via Bank

Make a transfer according to the invoice instructions, using one of the official accounts (examples):

- Bank Mandiri – Account No.: 1390010724486 a/n Gian Mokhammad Ramadhan
- Bank BCA – Account No.: 0461444741 a/n Gian Mokhammad Ramadhan
- Bank BTPN – Account No.: 90014798709 a/n Gian Mokhammad Ramadhan

(Account numbers can change at any time; always follow those listed on the latest invoice.)

After the transfer:
- Submit **payment confirmation** as instructed in the email/invoice (usually via support email or a confirmation form).

## 5. Activate and Download the Product

After payment is confirmed:

1. The team will activate your account and subscription (manual process after reading the confirmation email).
2. Once active, log in to the member area:  
   https://member.kentooz.com/member
3. In the member page, you can:
   - View the list of purchased products.
   - Download the theme files and accompanying plugins.

If the account is not active after some time:
- Check your email again (including SPAM) for follow‑up information.
- [Contact Idtheme/Kentooz support](https://www.idtheme.com/kontak-kami/) and include proof of payment.
